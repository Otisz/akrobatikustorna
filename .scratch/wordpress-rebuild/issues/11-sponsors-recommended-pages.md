# 11: Sponsors and Recommended Pages

**What to build:** The Site Owner adds a Sponsor with a logo and link so supporters are credited without a
developer, and adds or removes a Recommended Page so the club's outbound links stay current.

Both stay separate post types rather than collapsing into an options page, because "add a sponsor" matches a
list in the Site Owner's mental model better than a nested field group does.

**Blocked by:** 02, 03.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Sponsor is a custom post type with a name, logo, and URL, and no public URL of its own
- [ ] Sponsor logos appear on the public site, each linking to the sponsor's site
- [ ] Recommended Page is a custom post type with a name and URL, listed at `/ajanlott-oldalak`
- [ ] Adding or removing either is reflected publicly with no developer involvement
- [ ] Owner journey test: sign in as the Site Owner, add a Sponsor with a logo, see it credited on the public
      site
