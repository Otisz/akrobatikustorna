# 10: Video gallery at `/galeria`

**What to build:** The Site Owner adds a Video to the gallery by pasting a YouTube link, without needing to
understand embeds, so recordings of competitions appear without a developer. A visitor watches gallery Videos
without leaving the site.

**Blocked by:** 02, 03.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Video is a custom post type whose gallery resolves at `/galeria`
- [ ] A Video has a title and a YouTube identifier the Site Owner supplies by pasting a link
- [ ] Videos play in place on `/galeria`
- [ ] Owner journey test: sign in as the Site Owner, add a Video by pasting a YouTube link, see it playable in
      the gallery
